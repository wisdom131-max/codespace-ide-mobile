# Source Control / History / Trash: fresh source-only group audit

**State:** app and checked-out VS Code clone read; no device test run. `READ A/path:N` is 1-based in `android/app/src/main/java/com/codespace/ide/`, and `READ V/path:N` is 1-based in `vscode-src/` (repo root: `extensions/` and `src/vs/`). This group owns the SCM stack end to end: `scm/` (GitCommandExecutor, GitService, ScmState, ScmModels), the `SourceControlPane` UI with its dialogs (branches, merge, diff, history, tags, .gitignore, graph, clone, publish, repo browser), `TimelinePanel`, the version-history engine (`util/VersionHistoryV2` + the Explorer 20s capture loop), the Local History and Trash dialogs, and `GitHubAuth` device-flow sign-in. Trash mechanics (`WorkspaceManager.moveToTrash`/`restoreFromTrash`) and the Explorer delete flows were audited by the Explorer group (EX01-EX07) and are referenced, not re-audited. Editor buffer/refresh internals, the AI chat apply pipeline, Terminal process semantics, and Problems/build are boundaries. Nothing here is a device outcome or a code-fix authorization.
